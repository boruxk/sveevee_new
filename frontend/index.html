sveevee

sveevee helps people find local ads, business pages, community pages, products, services, events, ratings, and direct chat near them.

Visitors can search by city and neighborhood without registration. Registered users can create a profile, post local ads, build business and community pages, add store products and services, publish events, rate public pages, and start direct chats with other users.

Google Sign-In is optional. It is used only to create or access a sveevee account with the user's Google email and basic profile information.
